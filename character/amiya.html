Amiya's Infomation

Amiya is a 5★ Rhodes Island Core Caster Operator in Arknights and one of the main characters of the Main Theme. Amiya herself is also a boss in the Babel event and one of the possible final bosses of Sarkaz's Furnaceside Fables since its Expansion II under the name "Amiya", Furnace's Finale.
